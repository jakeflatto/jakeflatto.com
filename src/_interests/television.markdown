### **Nothing is more important than television, and no one more important than the people who make that television!**
{: .text-purple}

\- J. Goddamn D. Goddamn Salinger (Bojack Horseman - Season 2, Episode 8)
{: .text-teal style="text-align: right;"}

All of the reasons I love [film](/interests/film/) have also led me to love television as well. I'm grateful to have grown up through the birth and evolution of the golden age of television. 

While I've become disillusioned with the fractured state of streaming today, we are lucky to be treated to a wealth of prestige television as a result. And with that, I've come to appreciate the medium of television even more than the medium of film.

The possbilities for rich, layered storytelling resulting from the combination of:
1. The episodic nature of the medium
2. The extended amount of screen time available
3. The cinematic care that comes with prestige production

fascinates and delights me to no end. I love crawling down the rabbit hole of cyclic re-watching, and uncovering deeper layers of a show.
