I've been lucky enough to travel to 2 continents that aren't my own so far, and I hope one day to visit all of them.

My first trip abroad (as an adult) was a brief sojourn across the pond back in 2016, and since then, I've been passionate about international travel. I had some amazing experiences, and took many wonderful pictures.

My most recent trip was in 2023, and I'm aching for another. For now, I'm enjoying documenting thoughts and pictures from my previous trips. I hope you enjoy exploring them, and that they may inspire travel ideas of your own.
